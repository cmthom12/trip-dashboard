Travelers

Roster claim lock—

While this is off (the default), anyone who reaches the sign-in page can claim any unclaimed traveler name by picking a PIN. Turn it on once everyone has signed in at least once: unclaimed names are then refused, with the same message a wrong PIN gets, so the page gives nothing away. People who already have a PIN are unaffected.

Trip Setup

Active trip—

Paste a complete trip-data JSON below (see BUILD_WITH_AI.md), hit Validate, then Import. Importing replaces the trip for everyone; PINs and votes for names that stay in the family are kept.

Reset PIN: clears that person's PIN and signs out their devices; they simply pick a new PIN at next sign-in. Remove: same, plus clears any login lockout. Neither touches votes, notes or lists.
